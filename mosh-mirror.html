how it works

Every frame is coded as 16×16 blocks: skip, inter (a motion vector plus a DCT residual) or intra (raw DCT). Keyframes are all intra. MOSH throws keyframes and intra blocks away, so your motion keeps dragging stale pixels around. Stamps put a different picture in the decoder that the encoder doesn't know about. Radiation flips bits in the stream, and each row is a slice, so a hit garbles the rest of its row.
