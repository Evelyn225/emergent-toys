# ASCII City: implemented archipelago

The approved archipelago is now playable locally. Do not repeat the city transforms. Do not merge, push, deploy or publish unless the user requests it. Preserve unrelated `yomi-mods/` work.

## Current world

Runtime layout is `archipelago-v3`, save schema 5. The native storage grid is 1024 square; the finite, nonwrapping chart is independently 864 by 512. Distant ocean normalization remains separate. Larger storage does not generate more city blocks or pedestrians.

- Two irregular inhabited city islands retain the original 2,770 lots, 911 apartment lots, 1,100 pedestrians, neighborhoods, landmarks and courier routes. Seven whole edge-neighborhood translations provide the varied silhouette the user requested. The city does not need to follow every coastal bend.
- The original airport remains offshore north, on its separate L-shaped causeway. Two raised road bridges span the central shipping channel. The lighthouse has its own footbridge; the fair pier ends over water and does not connect to it.
- Fern Island is east of the city, reached by boat or air. It has woods, low hills, marked trails, a waterfall/stream, a western landing, fishing water, a tackle box, a campfire, and a real east-west 660 m rough strip. The strip was enlarged from the provisional 350-450 m sketch and has low approach corridors after actual flight checks.
- Fern House costs $6,800 at its front door. Its separately authored 24 by 24 metre interior has a bedroom, bathroom, kitchen/dining area, living room, workshop wing, shared 120-item closet and an exit to a real outdoor sea-facing terrace. Property ID is `fern-house`. Workshop equipment, food and decor use the normal persistent systems.
- Cabin Cove, Cormorant Rock, Crescent Sands and Juniper Isle have real shared shorelines/terrain and usable boat landings. Cabin Cove's cabin is now an unlocked public fishing shelter with its own 12 by 10 metre timber interior, benches, tackle rack, rope, gear chest and readable visitors' log. Juniper House is private: knock at the front door for three wooden knocks and no answer. Door plaques distinguish SHELTER and PRIVATE. Cormorant Rock is a rocky fishing outcrop; Crescent Sands has an actual inlet/crescent and driftwood. Extra small-house purchases were not in scope.
- The map labels Shotengai before repeated district labels, so it remains visible in the fitted overview. Map fit accounts for the directory panel and phone controls. Search exposes the islands, landing, strip, house and falls; remote details can guide the player to the mainland marina or airport. Offshore destinations receive boat/air access guidance, without a false mainland walking route.
- Hired boats remain moored ashore and survive reload. Saving during an owned/hired boat trip resumes at that boat; owned aircraft resume an active flight at its actual position. Helicopter hover saves retain a running rotor. Saving inside a home records its actual outdoor door.

Boat travel now has a separate cruise throttle: hold Shift with forward throttle on desktop, or push the phone stick fully forward. Normal W/partial-stick throttle retains its original docking speeds. Cruise speeds are speedboat 4.8, cruiser 3.1 and sailboat 1.4 cells/second; normal speeds remain 1.8 / 1.25 / .85. Releasing cruise eases down, S/back-stick brakes more strongly from cruise, and steering becomes gentler at speed. Translation and rotation use small hull sweeps so fast boats still meet piers, supports, coastlines and traffic. Rental instructions and both HUD layouts explain the controls.

The same control-driven marina-to-Fern route now takes approximately 1 minute 54 seconds including departure and braking, versus 4 minutes 8 seconds with the previous code (a roughly 54 percent reduction). Return to the marina is approximately 1 minute 56 seconds. Ordinary throttle still takes approximately 4 minutes 8 seconds. The controller now brakes at arrivals using the controls instead of assigning zero velocity. These measurements isolate the rental from ambient boat traffic. The airport-to-Fern flight remains approximately two and a half minutes with the conservative test approach. Geography, save coordinates and aircraft balance were not changed for this pacing pass.

## Source ownership and geometry

`tools/city-manifest.json` concatenates classic shared-scope scripts. Rebuild `ascii-city.bundle.js` with `node tools/build-city.cjs` after source edits. Pure scripts must remain DOM-free at load; `test/helpers/load-city.cjs` intentionally constructs the original 256-square source city. Browser bootstrap activates the archipelago.

- `world-layout.js`: source-to-world transforms, city coast fields, independent storage/chart dimensions. `citySourcePoint` includes all contour translations; adding (+128,+96/+144) manually is insufficient. Source place IDs never change.
- `world-bootstrap.js`: moves/reindexes old content once after all decorators, then builds native islands, buckets and terrain before save loading. `CITY_LAYOUT_CHECKPOINT` records the original counts; the new house adds one native place afterward.
- `islands.js`: native island records, shore fields, low strip/landing pads, trail heights and surface styles. Shore and trail fields cache at 5 m spacing. World collision, boats, rendering and the map query the same shoreline.
- `islands-content.js`: native cabins, woods, benches, fishing/camp fixtures, Fern House identity and its independently authored interior, waterfall and scenery. Native content does not enter the old coordinate relocation pass.
- `islandBuildingUse` / `islandBuildingPrompt` in `islands-content.js` share the island door interactions. Cabin Cove and Juniper's actual front doors are in `ISLAND_CABINS`. The generic shop-wall fallback excludes all island cabins, including Fern House: another wall cannot enter a generic shop/apartment lobby or describe a private house as FOR LEASE. Shelter entry records its outdoor door immediately so a save inside cannot strand the player in the city. Public shelter has no home storage/workshop or purchase record. Nearby trees are cleared around the small cabin entrances. The map lists the public shelter under Things to do; touch buttons say Knock and Read.
- `terrain.js`, `terrain-render.js`: triangulated 5 m ground shared with walking, aircraft, vegetation and effects. Roads/buildings/landing furniture stay level. Native slopes are capped at 28 percent; trails remain physically navigable.
- `world-bridges.js`, `world-bridges-render.js`: graded road decks at 16.5 m with water below and solid supports. Keep the tested distinction between deck and water, including boat disembarking.
- `aircraft.js`, `save.js`: aircraft follow terrain while taxiing and collide with actual map buildings. Save loading restores owned flights and their pilot together. Legacy source-only coastal plane serialization retains its stand recovery behavior.

## Save compatibility

Schema 1/2 coastal saves migrate through `citySourcePoint`; schema 3 (`archipelago-v1`) additionally receives the contour moves. Schema 4 (`archipelago-v2`) already has the final city coordinates: only raw home/tag cell strides change from 512 to 1024. Interior/workshop coordinates and stable property IDs do not move. Current saves are idempotent.

Original bytes remain under `ascii-city-save-backup-v2-coastal-v1`, `ascii-city-save-backup-v3-archipelago-v1` or `ascii-city-save-backup-v4-archipelago-v2`, as appropriate. Earlier backups remain intact. Unknown/corrupt saves cannot be overwritten. Backup-quota failure loads progress but blocks overwriting until a backup succeeds.
